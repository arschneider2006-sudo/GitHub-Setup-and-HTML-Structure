Help the alien complete its mission before humans discover the UFO!

Mission Briefing

Your mission is to help an alien secretly abduct every cow by finding matching pairs hidden beneath the cards.

Be careful! Two Human cards are hidden somewhere in the deck. If humans spot the alien twice, the mission fails!

Instructions

  • Click two cards to flip them over.
  • Matching cards stay face-up.
  • Non-matching cards flip back over.
  • Human cards do NOT have a matching pair.
  • Each Human card revealed counts as one sighting.
  • After two sightings, the mission fails.
  • Find all 8 matching pairs before two sightings to win.
